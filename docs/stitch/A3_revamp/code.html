Executive summary

✓ Approved

The Study ·Scoop & Co is deciding which of three new ice cream flavours to launch: Salted Caramel Swirl, Mango Lassi and Cookie Dough Crunch.1 This study tested which flavour people would choose, and how they reacted to each.2 20 people took part in AI-led interviews, 8 parents and 12 adults without children.3

What we found ·Salted Caramel Swirl was chosen to take home more often than any other flavour.4 Mango Lassi drew the warmest first reactions on video but was the least chosen to take home.5 3 of 7 people who picked Cookie Dough Crunch first said in the locked survey they probably would not buy it.6 Parents and adults without children picked different flavours first, though both groups are small.7 Price expectations and when people would eat it were raised by too few people to draw a finding.8

✎Researcher's implications · written by you, not AI

Client asked about Caramel in the brief. Lead with it in the debrief.
