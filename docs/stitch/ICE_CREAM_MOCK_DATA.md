# Ice Cream Flavour Concept Test: mock data for design

The target data for the three tabs of **Emotion & behaviour** (A2): Themes, Concepts, Tensions. It is one data set and it adds up: every number below is worked out from the people table in section 0, by the product's own rules, and checked by script.

How to use it: design each tab from its section. Where a line is in quotes it is the text to show. Where a table has a column called "Chip", that is the small pill on the screen. Statuses and labels are exactly the product's.

**Rules that hold everywhere.** Counts are "n of N people", never shown as a share of 100. "Most" means more than half. A face is only ever "reads as". What respondents said, what the AI wrote, and what the researcher wrote are kept apart and labelled. Nothing is a score.

### 1.4 States to design (illustrations only)

**Out of date.** The data moved after an insight was accepted. Status chip **Out of date**; amber box on the Insight heading:

> The data changed after you decided.
> Was: 12 of 14 people were verbally positive about it, and their delivery backed it up.
> Now: 11 of 14 people were verbally positive about it, and their delivery backed it up.

Footer reads "Accepted by PM · 21 Nov 2026 · data v1 · out of date". Accept stays available (to accept the new sentence).

**Edit mode.** Insight becomes a text box. Hint: "You can change the wording. The numbers are locked to PulseAI’s." Buttons [Save wording] [Cancel].

> 12 of 14 people said good things about it, and their delivery backed it up.

If the numbers are changed, Save is off and a red line says: "The numbers are locked. Change the wording, not the figures." A figure shown as a share of 100 is refused by the same rule.

**Left out.** Status chip **Left out**; the sticky bar shows a grey line "Reason: Not what the brief asked." Footer "Left out by PM · 21 Nov 2026 · data v1". The reason is optional, except for a theme or concept with nothing to decide, where it is required.

**Nothing to decide** (a code with fewer than 3 people, e.g. “The kids wouldn’t eat it”, 2 people). Status chip grey **Nothing to decide**; Accept and Edit are off with the tip "Too few people to read a pattern"; Leave out needs a reason.

**How to read this** (one popover next to "Expand all"; two short sections)

*What the labels mean*

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Consistent | More than half of the people with a position matched. |
| Mixed | Neither side has more than half, including when many were hesitant. |
| Gap | More than half had no match. |
| No position | Nobody took a side. |
| Too few to read | Under 3 people, too few to read a pattern. |

| Did their delivery match their words? | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Matched | Nothing in how they sounded contradicted the words. |
| No match | How they sounded contradicted the words. |
| Hesitant | Nothing contradicted the words, but they hesitated a lot. Never counts toward Gap. |
| Split across codes | Their positions across codes point both ways and neither side is ahead. In the base, in no tile. |

| What people said (stance tag) | Meaning |
|---|---|
| For it | People who said this code were for it. |
| Against it | People who said this code were against it. |
| Just describes | People who said this code just described what happened. Nothing for delivery to match. |

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Suggested | PulseAI wrote this. Nothing is decided yet. |
| Accepted | You accepted this insight as written. |
| Edited | You accepted this insight with your own wording. The numbers are PulseAI’s. |
| Left out | You left this insight out of the report. |
| Out of date | The data changed after you decided. Look again. |
| Nothing to decide | Too few people to read a pattern. |

| Concept Match | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Agrees | What people said, did and showed point the same way. |
| Differs | Its rank moves two places or more between what people said, did and showed, and the shares are clearly apart. |

*How it’s decided*

1. We look at people who spoke in favour or against it and compare how they sounded with what they said. Faces play no part.
2. No match if they spoke in favour and sounded tense or flat, or spoke against it and sounded animated.
3. More than half matched: Consistent. More than half had no match: Gap. Otherwise Mixed. Under 3 people: Too few to read.
4. Hesitating a lot, with nothing else off, shows as Hesitant and never counts toward Gap.
5. Each person counts once. With several codes in a theme, their positions are counted together.
