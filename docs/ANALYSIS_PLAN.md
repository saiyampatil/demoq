# Results Analysis: product plan

Our product decisions for the Results Analysis module. `docs/ANALYSIS_BRIEF.md` is the founder's original brief, for context. Where they differ, this plan wins.

## Principles

- AI proposes, the Researcher approves. Nothing reaches the client unless the Researcher accepted it.
- Every claim traces back to a quote, timestamp and session.
- Honest denominators: always "n of N", never a bare percentage. N can differ per question.

## Two surfaces

- **Analysis workspace (Researcher):** codes, confidence, signals, tensions, regenerate. Work in progress.
- **Client report (client):** only approved findings and outputs. A published, versioned snapshot. Never shows unapproved codes, excluded sessions' clips, or raw confidence.

## Steps (analysis sub-stepper)

| # | Step | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Base | What the analysis is built on: accepted sessions only, transcript versions, speaker separation, per-question denominators, caveats, guide gaps. The Researcher approves it before coding. |
| 1 | Codebook | Reviewed at code level, not chunk level. Guide-anchored and emergent codes shown separately. |
| 2 | Themes | Built only from accepted codes. |
| 3 | Signals and tensions | Behaviour described, not diagnosed ("paused 2.8 s"). Analysed at key moments only, coverage always shown, never used to exclude. Facial analysis is on hold pending a consent and legal decision. |
| 4 | Findings | Structured by the guide's objectives. AI draft and Researcher draft. Every sentence cited. Weak or unanswered questions flagged. |
| 5 | Outputs | Theme cards with video (the hero output), showreel, word cloud, podcast. |
| 6 | Lenses | Re-read the coded data through a framework. |
| 7 | Publish | Report v1 snapshot. If a Review decision changes later, the workspace shows the analysis as stale. Published versions never change silently. |

**Always-on side panel:** Ask (cited answers only), Numbers (segment differences labelled "directional"), Theme map, Real-time highlights.

## Phases

1. **Core:** Base, Codebook, Themes, Findings, Client report.
2. **Signals and tensions, theme videos, showreel.**
3. **Word cloud, podcast, lenses, compare studies, translation.**

## Demo study

Pet Food Subscription Churn (Complete, 22 completed sessions, 20 accepted). Do not fix its guide: the Base screen is meant to surface its gaps.
