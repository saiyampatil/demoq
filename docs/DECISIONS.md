Reason: issues were handed out round-robin, so they didn't match what Review showed for the same session.

## Stage 2 prep: four fixes and the time zone

The zone is a field on the study record (`timeZone`, default America/New_York), read through `lib/timeZone.ts`; the label comes from the zone ("ET", "CT", "PT", or a UTC offset). Reason: a bare "11:20 AM" is ambiguous, and R23 (Study settings) will edit the zone, so nothing may hard-code Eastern. Date-only values carry no label.
- Fix 1: progress is always two numbers, confirmed (Accepted) and pending (Under review), with Excluded removed: R18's header ("3 confirmed, 9 pending review, 12 of 24 toward target"), the funnel's Completed row, the quota bars (accepted solid, under review hatched), the Quotas tab (separate Confirmed and Pending review columns), the region spread and the Studies dashboard ("3 confirmed, 9 pending of 24"). Reason: an Under review session is not confirmed progress, and a single "12 of 24" hid that 9 of the 12 could still be excluded.
- Fix 1: pace and the verdict use confirmed plus pending and say so ("Counts accepted plus under-review sessions, with the 2 excluded removed"); the verdict adds a warning when pending is more than half of progress. Reason: the Researcher should not read pending sessions as safe.
- Fix 2: every session has its own ID, `P-2041-1`, `P-2041-2`, separate from the participant ID `P-2041`. The participant ID is the main label everywhere; "Session 2 of 2" is shown only for a participant who has more than one session (R18 Sessions, R19, R21, R22, Needs you). URLs use the session ID, and a bare participant ID resolves to their latest (or their reviewable) session. Reason: a participant can come back (retry, resume, reschedule, and later escalation) and each visit needs its own record, recording and review.
- Fix 2: respondent history is grouped under the participant: R19 shows "All sessions for P-2044", R22's Session details shows the same list. Counts of people (funnel, Needs you, verdict) use the participant's latest session; counts of sessions use all of them. Reason: P-2048 retried must count once in the funnel, not twice.
- Fix 2: seed data gains two returning participants: P-2044 (technical error, then a completed second session) and P-2046 (left part-way, then completed). 25 participants, 27 sessions. Reason: the multi-session case has to exist in the mock to be checked.
- Fix 2: consent is recorded once per participant, in their first session; later sessions reuse it. Reason: the consent log counts people, not visits.
- Fix 3: R22 for a session that isn't finished redirects to the participant's R19 record with the line "Still in fieldwork, not ready for review."; only an ID that matches no participant at all shows "Session not found". Reason: "not found" told the Researcher something false about a real person.
- Fix 4: the test driver waits on state: `goto` waits for the page load and for the app to mount, a click on an element that isn't drawn yet retries until it is, and every assertion polls until true (4 s) instead of reading once; it also waits for the previous Chrome to exit. Reason: three flows passed alone and failed in the chain because they sampled the page before it had finished drawing.
- Stage 2: the verdict is computed (On track, Behind pace, At risk), with one sentence and one action; At risk means the people who can still finish, plus the reserve, can't reach the target. Reason: R18 answers "am I on track?" before anything else.
- Stage 2: Needs you sits directly under the verdict and lists only unfinished sessions, each with participant, what happened, how long ago and one inline action (Assign moderator, Resend link, Retry, Approve replacement, Reschedule), most urgent first. An action takes the row out of the list, writes a session event and an activity entry, and Retry opens a new session. Reason: "what needs me?" must be answerable and actionable on one screen.
- Stage 2: the eight counter tiles are replaced by one funnel (Invited, Opened, Consented, Started, Completed) counted by participant. Withdrew and No-show branch off where they happened, waiting people are listed at their stage, and a line adds it all up ("25 invited = 14 completed + 3 left the study + 8 still in progress"). Reason: every number must add up, so nobody goes unaccounted for.
- Stage 2: a coverage alert appears in the funnel when invited plus reserve can't reach the target: "Invited 25 for a target of 24, 0 in reserve. 3 have dropped out (2 excluded, 1 withdrew), so at most 22 can finish. You can't finish without inviting more." with an Invite more action. Reason: the count that matters is who can still finish, not who was invited.
- Stage 2: Overview keeps the verdict, Needs you, the funnel, quota bars and one line each for interview length and the review link; pace, technical issues and consent cards move out. The tabs are Overview, Sessions, Quotas, Issues, Consent log. Reason: the Overview was a wall of equal-weight cards. ## Stage 2 fixes

- Fix 1 (funnel): every participant is in exactly one of four places: completed, withdrew, no-show, still in progress. Excluded sessions are inside completed ("Inside the 14: 3 accepted, 9 under review, 2 excluded"). The equation reads "25 invited = 14 completed (3 accepted + 9 under review + 2 excluded) + 1 withdrew + 2 no-show + 8 still in progress." The funnel numbers themselves already summed to 25; what double-counted was the coverage alert, which wrote "3 have dropped out (2 excluded, 1 withdrew)" while the funnel's "3 left" was 2 no-shows and 1 withdrew. Two different groups of three shared one number. Both now come from the same four places, and a test asserts the funnel sums to invited (in the equation and in the stage lines) at 25 and again at 26 after a replacement. Reason: nobody may be counted twice or go unaccounted for.
- Fix 2 (ceiling): the most a study can reach is counting sessions plus people still in progress (plus any reserve): 12 + 8 = 20. No-shows and people who withdrew are not "still in progress", so they are not in the ceiling (the old 22 counted the two no-shows). The verdict reads "Even if everyone still open finishes, you'd end on 20." and its action is computed: "Recruit at least 4 more" (2 + 2), never typed in. Reason: a person who never showed up isn't a plan.
- Fix 3 (excluded): excluded is its own line ("2 excluded in review (replacements needed)") and never "left the study". Withdrew and no-show are the people who left. Reason: an excluded interview was done and is a quota slot to refill; the person didn't leave.
- Fix 4 (per cohort): the shortfall is computed per cohort against its quota from confirmed plus pending plus still in progress plus reserve, and the verdict and the coverage alert name the cohort ("Younger, 25–34 is short by 2 and Older, 35–44 is short by 2"). Overall totals are the sums and sit underneath. A cohort with spare people can't cover another. Reason: the client's quotas are per segment, so a total of 20 can still hide one empty segment.
- Fix 5 (recruit): "Invite more" is replaced by "Recruit for [cohort]". It reopens screening for that cohort only, with the same screener and audience rules, so no new version is made (the dialog says so). In the mock one eligible applicant then arrives and goes to the reserve list; Needs you shows "Approve replacement" for them (paired with the participant who withdrew from that cohort, or as a row of their own); approving invites them as a new participant with a first session, and the study's numbers move (Younger short by 2, then 1 in reserve, then 4 still in progress). Reason: it shows the whole chain: shortfall, screening, reserve, invitation.
- Fix 6 (retry resumes): a retry still gets a new session ID, but it loads the previous session's last saved state (4.7): questions asked, covered questions, stimuli shown, locked survey answers and the conversation so far. R19 shows a "Carried over from P-2048-1" card, tags carried answers and stimuli, and marks the handoff point in the timeline. P-2044-2 and P-2046-2 are seeded this way and so is every new retry. Later sessions also show the consent given in session 1. Reason: a participant who comes back must never restart from a blank page.

The name is read from the filters (gender, age range, skin type) and follows them until the Researcher renames it; "All participants" and "Cohort 2" no longer exist anywhere. Reason: the client's target is one group of people, and a second segment was an invented quota the PRD never asked for.

Add cohort, Duplicate, Remove, per-cohort participant numbers and the allocation bar are gone, and Confirm no longer checks that cohorts add up. Reason: with one cohort there is nothing to allocate; the participant count is the study's target.

R9's "Cohort fill" is now one bar, "Target fill" (eligible N of target, reserve count); the Cohort column and per-cohort wording are gone. Reason: only an Eligible applicant is a candidate for an interview, and the target is the one number to fill.

Audiences saved with two cohorts are migrated on load to one (the first cohort's filters, age widened to cover the others) and saved fieldwork is rebuilt from the new seed (`SESSION_SEED` 5). Otherwise counting below expected-by-now is Behind pace, otherwise On track. Reason: the old text mixed pace into a coverage problem and could print an "expected by now" number as the denominator of "done".

- Recruit for [cohort] is now one "Recruit {gap} more" button that reopens screening for the study's one cohort (same screener, same audience rules, no new version); `recruiting` on the fieldwork doc is a single record and Overview shows one "Screening is open" strip. Reason: one cohort means one screening to reopen.
- The funnel still sums to invited: 25 = 14 completed + 1 withdrew + 2 no-show + 8 still in progress, asserted by the test at 25 and again after a replacement. Reason: nobody may be counted twice or go unaccounted for.

## Part B: reserve pool, sourcing top-up, real pause

- The target is a ledger of slots, each held or open. A slot is held by a counting interview (accepted or under review), by a participant who can still finish (invited, consented, in progress, booked, or with a human), or by a no-show while the fieldwork window is open. Reason: "who can still finish" and "is the gap open" must be one computed thing, not two screens guessing.
- A slot opens only when a session is excluded in review, a participant withdraws, a no-show is still a no-show after the window closes, or the Researcher gives up on a participant with a reason. A technical error, a participant who left part-way, an invalid link and (in Part C) an escalation waiting for a moderator keep their slot and appear in Needs you. Reason: those people can still finish, so replacing them would double-book the study.
- Consequence for the verdict: the two seeded no-shows hold their slots until Nov 17, 2026, so the seed now reads "Only 22 of 24 can finish. 2 more people are needed." (Part A said 20 and 4, which counted them as gone). The basis line says so: "12 counting (…) + 8 still open + 2 no-shows holding a slot until Nov 17, 2026 = 22." After the window closes the same two open their slots and it reads 20 and 4. Reason: the Part B rule is the later and more specific one. Open question #86.
- The slot ledger is drawn on the Reserve tab: one square per slot (counting solid, still-open hatched, no-show outlined, open dashed), a legend that adds up to the target, and a list of the slots that have opened and why. Reason: the verdict says "2 more needed"; the ledger shows which slots those are.
- Replacement needs are the gap, never Needs you rows. "Approve replacement" and the reserve-candidate rows are gone from Needs you; a withdrawn participant is no longer a row. Needs you stays unfinished sessions only. Reason: a replacement is a study-level need, not a participant's problem.
- Needs you actions are renamed to what they do: Resume (left part-way), Reissue link (invalid or expired link), Retry (technical error), Resend link, Reschedule, Assign moderator. Reissue link still sends the link again for now; invalidating the old one is Part D. Reason: the Part B brief names Resume, Reissue and Assign.
- Give up and replace sits beside the primary action on a stuck row (technical error, left part-way, a link or permission problem that blocks the start). It needs a reason, logs who and when on the session and in the activity feed, opens the slot, and keeps the data. The funnel gains a fifth place, "given up", shown in the equation only when it is not zero, and at the stage the person had reached ("1 given up, slot opened"). Reason: the Researcher must be able to stop waiting for someone, and that has to be a decision with a name on it.
- The verdict action depends on the situation: reserve covers the gap, "Invite {gap} from reserve"; reserve covers part of it, "Invite {k} from reserve, {gap - k} more needed" plus "Source more"; reserve empty, "Source {gap} more". It is computed from the gap and the people who can be invited now (Available, not Re-screen needed). Reason: one button for the next best step.
- The Reserve tab lists everyone who passed screening and is not in an interview: order, applicant, contact, source (Manual or External), state, screened date and time, and status Available, Invited, Used, Expired or Re-screen needed. The default order is first in, first out by the screened time; "Invite first N" takes the next N in line, and any Available person can be invited from their row. The banner on top shows the gap and the reserve size. Reason: FIFO is fair by default, but the Researcher may need a particular person (for example a region).
- Used means an invited reserve person has started their session. Expired means an invitation sat unopened for 72 hours. The invited session still holds its slot and shows in Needs you ("Resend link"). Reason: the reserve list should say what became of each invitation. Open question #87.
- Reserve people only ever enter through screening. Applicants invited after launch are tagged `afterLaunch`; if they qualify they are Reserve, never Eligible and never straight into an interview, and R9's drawer says "Screened after the study launched…". Eligible applicants beyond the target are Reserve too. Reason: the pool a Researcher confirmed before launch is the interview pool; later people must wait to be invited.
- "Source more" opens R8 in top-up mode (the study is Live or Paused): the audience and screener are shown as locked to the release (versions named, "no new version is made"), the banner reads "Need {n} more eligible. Reserve has {k}." where n is the gap minus the reserve, and an estimate says how many applicants that takes from the observed pass rate ("about 5. So far 13 of 22 who answered the screener qualified (59%)"). A dev card "Simulate: N applicants arrive" adds N External applicants who answer the screener; some qualify and some don't. Back on R18 the reserve is updated. Reason: the Researcher needs to know how much sourcing to buy, and the loop has to run end to end without the panel.
- A reserve person screened under an older version of the audience reads "Re-screen needed", is not offered by Invite first or the verdict, and can't be invited. The check is the audience version now in force against the version they were screened under, so editing the audience after launch flags them and "Discard changes" clears the flag. Reason: they passed rules that no longer apply.
- Real pause. Pausing needs a reason and logs who and when (as before); resuming logs who and when. While paused, Invite from the reserve, Invite first, Source more, the verdict's invite and source buttons, and every Needs you action that sends a participant a link (Resend link, Reissue link, Resume, Retry, Reschedule) are disabled with the reason "Study is paused"; R8 disables Send screener invites and the arrivals simulator. Give up and replace, Assign moderator and reading everything still work. Reason: a pause that only draws a banner pauses nothing.
- Participants and pause: a dev control on R19, "Simulate: participant opens their link", stands in for the participant app. While paused it shows "This study is temporarily unavailable", starts nothing and logs it on the session; after Resume the same link opens. Another, "Simulate: running session takes its next turn", shows that a running session carries on while paused. Reason: the participant screens are not built, so the rule has to be demonstrable from the researcher side.
- Not built: auto-invite from the reserve when a slot opens. Default off; open question #88. A running session finishing (Completed) while paused is not simulated, because a completed session needs a review record, which comes with Part E.

## Part E: R22 decisions

- A session's review status is no longer stored or seeded: it is worked out from the history of what happened to it (`state/decisions.ts`). The last decision or reopen wins. Accept, Exclude and Reopen therefore change the status, and every count that reads outcomes moves with them: R18's progress line, the verdict, the funnel, the slot ledger, R21's cards and rows, the stepper's "awaiting" badge and the Studies dashboard. Reason: seeded decisions were fixed truth, so the review screens could not change the numbers they feed.
- The history is append-only and kept in the browser (`state/reviewState.ts`, `events`). Nothing is edited or erased: a reopen adds an entry, and the earlier decision stays in History on the record and in Activity. Seeded decisions (P-2062 accepted, P-2063 and P-2064 excluded) are the first history entries, so they can be reopened like any other. Reason: PRD 4.8 "every decision records the reviewer and rationale", and audit needs the whole trail.
- Auto-accept is a rule, not seed data: a completed session with no flags raised arrives Accepted, recorded as "Auto-accepted: no flags raised." by PulseAI (auto), 22 minutes after it completed. The rule reads flags raised, so a session whose flags are all dismissed is still flagged and stays Under review. The stale auto-accept for P-2061 (who never completed) is removed. Reason: unflagged sessions don't need review (4.8 Step 5), but the Researcher can reopen any of them.
- Accept modal: effect line "Counts towards the study target and is available in Results Analysis."; a summary grid (participant, moderator, release, both scores, no segment); a required note with a 500-character counter; if flags are on the session they are listed and "I've reviewed these flags" must be ticked. Accept needs the note and, when there are flags, the acknowledgement. Reason: PRD 4.8 "Accept with notes: a note is required"; the flags are decision support and must have been seen.
- Exclude modal: effect line "Frees one slot in the study target. The session stays visible for audit but is left out of analysis."; a required rationale, given as one or more ticked reasons, typed text, or both. Excluding opens a slot in the Part B ledger and the session leaves the target. Reason: 4.8 "Exclude: a rationale is required"; the ledger must show the slot at once.
- Request clarification: the recipient is the participant; the moderator is offered only when the session was run by a human (`?demo=human` on R22 shows that). A message is required and flags can be attached. The status stays Under review with the sub-state "Awaiting clarification", shown in R22's header and bar and in R21's row and Awaiting-review card ("1 awaiting clarification"). It keeps counting as pending. A dev button, "Simulate: the answer arrives", adds the answer to Activity and clears the sub-state. Reason: PRD 4.8 leaves "who is asked" open (#92); the participant is the default.
- Order replacement: it shows the next reserve candidate, first in first out, or says the reserve is empty and offers nothing to confirm. It takes two steps and one confirmation: the candidate, then a forced Accept or Exclude on the original session with that decision's own rules. Nothing is invited until the last button, so the session can never be left Under review with a replacement already sent. Closing the dialog at any step changes nothing. It is blocked while the study is paused ("Study is paused"). Reason: 4.8 "the original session is then accepted or excluded", and an invitation is an invitation (paused blocks it).
- Every modal shows its computed effect before it confirms (PRD 4.8 Q3): Accept, Exclude, Clarify, Replacement, Reopen and Dismiss. The numbers come from the same functions the screens use (`effectOf`), run on a copy: confirmed, pending, counting toward the target, and open slots before and after. Replacement runs the invitation too, so "exclude and replace" shows the gap staying put. Reason: the Researcher should never find out what a decision did after the fact.
- Accepted and Excluded sessions: the action bar becomes a read-only decision record (the decision, who, when with the time zone, the note and any reasons) with "Reopen for review". Reopen needs a reason, shows its effect (an exclusion's slot being taken back), logs who and when, and returns the session to Under review. Reason: 4.8 "Researcher able to reopen any".
- Dismiss flag: each active flag on an Under review session has Dismiss, which needs a reason. The flag stays listed, struck through, with who, when and why; it drops out of the score (the points come back), and the flag count reads "(2, 1 dismissed)". R21 shows the recalculated score. Dismissed flags no longer mark the recording's timeline. Reason: flags are decision support; a Researcher who disagrees with one should be able to say so without erasing what the system found.
- R22 gains an Activity tab: every entry (auto-accept, decisions, reopens, clarification requests and answers, replacements, dismissed flags) with who and when, oldest first, and no edit or delete control. Reason: the append-only trail the decision record points at.
- Seeding a new stage ("Reset seed" or a Study stage change) clears the review history, because decisions belong to the seed sessions they were made on. Reason: a fresh seed must not inherit decisions from the last one. Decisions are keyed by session ID, so a second study with the same seed session IDs would share them (open question #93).
- Shared Modal: a body that scrolls (58% of the window) and a wider size for the decision dialogs. Reason: the Accept dialog with flags and a note is taller than the old fixed panel.
- Not built here: a Completed session being created by a running one (so a replacement who finishes enters Review). It needs a review record generator, which belongs with the participant interview. Order replacement invites the candidate; their review comes later.

- Language: a person whose language isn't the study's shows a warning in the table and on R15. It warns, it doesn't block.
- Adding or removing a person is an edit to the setup, so on an approved guide it makes a new version, like any other moderator edit. What happens next (accepts, declines, PulseAI confirms) is not a guide edit and never makes a version. Resend only refreshes the sent time, and a declined person is asked again.
- R12 asks for at least one moderator of any status to continue. R15 blocks launch on a human-led study until one has accepted ("No moderator has accepted yet"). It warns, without blocking, when coverage is short, when invitations are still pending, or when a language doesn't match.
- Dev controls (simulate accept, decline, PulseAI confirms assignment) are in the Screen jump panel, in a "Moderator invitations" section that appears on R12 (`src/dev/ModeratorSimulator.tsx`). "Confirm assignment" matches each request to a roster moderator who speaks the language.
- "Someone from my team" says the person can set their own availability and see only their own sessions in the Moderator dashboard, which is not built yet (Phase 6).

## Issues tab removed from the Fieldwork command center (R18)

- **Why:** the Issues tab was a record, not a tool. Rows that need the Researcher are already in Needs you. Issues on one interview belong on the session page and in Review. Its "Who acts" and "Resolves when" columns never changed what a Researcher does. The one thing worth keeping is the pattern: the same problem happening to several people.
- R18's tabs are now Overview, Sessions, Reserve. An old `?tab=issues` link lands on the Overview. The issue log, its Open / Resolved filter, the three summary cards and the issue-type table are gone, and so is the Issues count badge.
- **Supersedes** the earlier entries that describe the Issues tab (the tab lists, "Issues tab: three cards", "The Issues tab is a read-only record", the Issues column note on Sessions, and the line about self-fixed problems being counted there). Those entries stay above as history.
- **What stays, because other screens read it:** the issue data on each session (`TechIssue`), `ISSUE_META` and `ISSUE_FIX`, and `issueSummary` in `state/selectors.ts`. `issueSummary` has no screen now. It is kept so the full log is available as data for a future export. Needs you rules, the PulseAI 2-hour timeout row, the session page and Review flags are unchanged.
- **Session page:** the Technical issues card stays as the per-interview record: the issue, when it happened, how it resolved, and who resolved it, with the time zone.
- **Sessions tab:** an "Issues" filter (any session that had an issue, open or resolved) so a Researcher can still find them. There is no Issues column. "Has open issues" stays.
- **Repeating-problem line (Overview):** one line between the verdict and Needs you, only when the same problem has reached 3 or more different participants in the study (`REPEATING_PROBLEM_MIN_PEOPLE` in `src/config/repeatingProblem.ts`). People are counted, not sessions. Each problem has a short suggested check, kept in the same file: microphone or camera blocked (pre-session instructions), browser or device unsupported (supported browser notice), link expired or invalid (invitation window and link settings), connection lost (interview length and stability), stimulus failed to load (stimulus file in Interview materials), consent or recording failed (report to PulseAI support). Issue types not in that list never make the line.
- If two problems repeat, only the one that affects more people is named, with "and 1 other problem" linking to the second. The line links to Sessions filtered to the people behind it. It is neutral, amber only while fieldwork is behind pace, and hidden when nothing repeats, when fieldwork is paused, and when it is closed. There is no empty state.
- Researchers unblock participants; they do not confirm technical fixes. Nothing here adds a way to mark an issue fixed.
- Dev: Screen jump has a "Repeating problem (mock)" section on R18 that gives several participants the same resolved issue (and can remove it again). The simulated clock and moderator no-show controls are in the same panel.
- `tests/issues-tab.mjs` was removed with the tab. `tests/issues.mjs` and `tests/needs-you-s2.mjs` still open `?tab=issues` for part of their checks, so those steps no longer apply.


## Launch is one screen (R15)

- Launch (R15) now holds everything: the entry contract, one row for the interview test runs, and Authorize launch. The preflight and authorize screen (R17) is hidden, and its old address lands on R15. The Launch step no longer has a sub-stepper.
- **Entry contract:** the same rows as before, each with Edit, read live from the earlier steps. The compiled study plan no longer has its own section; it compiles in the background when Launch opens, and shows only when it is compiling or has errors (with a Go to guide button).
- **Test runs:** one row with how it stands (Not run, In progress, Completed, Issues found, Out of date) and one button that runs the next combination that still needs running. A study with several languages or devices has "Show every combination", which opens the language-by-device table. The rule is unchanged: every combination has to be completed or have its issues reported.
- **Preflight checks** (two critical, four overridable) only appear, under "Fix before launch", when one fails or has been overridden. Critical ones can't be overridden. A failed check that belongs to the test runs scrolls to them.
- **Authorize launch:** study, language and one button. Underneath it, the first thing in the way ("A test run must be completed."), with the rest in a tooltip. Relaunching after a change shows the release number too.
- The sticky Previous/Continue bar is gone from Launch: there is nothing to continue to.

## Results Analysis: the Codebook replaces the Base (A1)

- **No Base step.** Review already decides which sessions are analysed, so a second approval was redundant. The Base screen and `state/analysis.ts` are removed. A1 is now the Codebook at `analysis/codebook`. The analysis stepper is Codebook, Signals & tensions, Findings, Outputs, Lenses, Publish; Themes is a tab, not a step.
- **What the Base did that stays:** a line under the title says what the codebook is built on (sessions accepted in Review, excluded ones left out, corrected transcripts used). N is worked out per guide question, so a question that wasn't always asked shows "Asked 14 of 20" and its codes count against 14.
- **One screen, three tabs.** Codes (founder brief steps 1 to 3), Themes (step 4), Change log (step 5). Export to Excel is always in the header: four sheets, Codes, Quotes, Themes and Change log, with statuses as columns.
- **Codes tab:** codes are grouped by guide question, because Findings are written per question; new codes have their own group. Each row shows people as "n of N" (distinct people, never quote counts) and confidence as High, Medium or Low. The default view is To review, most-said first. Accepting moves to the next code.
- **Code panel:** definition (editable), who said it with a dog vs cat split labelled Directional, quotes (each with participant, time, the question it answered, and Play, which opens Review at that moment), similar codes said by the same people (a likely merge), and recent changes.
- **Decisions:** Accept; Reject (reason required, leaves any theme, can be restored); Rename (why optional); Merge (reason required; quotes move, the source stays as Merged); Remove a quote (reason required). Every one is logged with who and when.
- **Consistency (step 3):** after a rename, merge or definition edit, PulseAI offers excerpts from other transcripts that may now fit, each with Add or Skip. Skipped ones aren't offered again.
- **Accept high-confidence:** one button that lists the suggested High-confidence codes, each untickable. Each accept is logged on its own.
- **Themes tab:** only accepted codes can be placed; a code sits in one theme. A theme counts each person once. Codes move by drag and drop or a menu (for keyboard users). PulseAI suggests themes for codes not in a theme yet (AI-generated, Accept, Edit first or Dismiss). Each theme shows which guide questions it draws from.
- **Review changes after coding:** counts follow Review straight away. A banner names who changed and offers "Update the codebook", which makes it permanent and logs it.
- **Quotes are real lines.** Every coded quote is written into that participant's transcript at the turn it cites (`placeTranscriptLines` in `mock/review.ts`), with the guide question on the turn before. P-3107's price quote is a corrected line, so it only reads that way in the reviewed transcript.
- **Statuses:** Suggested, Accepted, Rejected, Merged, added as their own vocabulary.
- **Left out:** the optional second coding pass. Logged as an open question.
- `tests/analysis-base.mjs` was removed with the Base screen.

## Stimulus library: a concept card is a picture and a description

- **New concept card** now has a title, an optional picture and a description, so a card is one unit: what the participant sees and reads together. A card without a picture is still allowed (text only). The picture takes the same image types and 200 MB limit as an upload; a video is refused with a message.
- **Upload files** is for raw images and videos only. A PDF is no longer a concept card: it shows the unsupported-format error, which points to New concept card. The PRD's "concept cards" are covered by the in-app card.

- The seeded cards (Concepts A to C in both studies) are unchanged: text only, "1 page".
