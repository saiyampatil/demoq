# Fieldwork demo (R18)

## Set up
1. Open the prototype, then **Screen jump** (bottom left) and pick **Live: demo** under Study stage.
2. Open the Moisturizer Concept Study, then **Fieldwork**.
3. To run it again, pick **Reset seed**, then **Live: demo** again. Inviting from the reserve and assigning a moderator change the data, so reset between runs.

Not part of the demo: Pause and Terminate.

## What the demo study has
- At risk, 2 people short, and a reserve of 4 people, so the button reads **Invite all 4 from reserve**.
- Needs you: 7 rows, the first one an escalation waiting for a moderator (P-2042).
- A repeating problem: Connection lost for 3 people.
- 11 finished sessions waiting for review.
- Five escalations, ending five ways: a standby moderator joined (P-2043), the Researcher assigned one (P-2045), the participant ended the interview (P-2050), the participant booked a follow-up (P-2047), and one still waiting (P-2042).

## Script (about 8 minutes)

**1. Overview (1 min)**
"Four numbers: how many interviews count toward the target, how many are waiting for my review, days left, and average length. The verdict says At risk: counting everyone still in progress, we would finish 22 of 24. The boxes are the slots, one per interview."

**2. Needs you (2 min)**
"Only what I can act on, oldest first. Someone consented but never started, so I resend their link. This link expired, so I reissue it. If I decide someone won't come back, I give up and replace them, which opens their slot. A row leaves when the next thing happens. Nothing is marked resolved by hand."

**3. The reserve (2 min)**
"We recruit more people than the target on purpose. The ones we screened and approved but didn't need yet wait in the reserve, in the order they arrived. We use it when a slot opens: someone is excluded in review, withdraws, I give up on them, or a no-show passes the window. Instead of recruiting again, I invite the next person who has already qualified."
- Open the **Reserve** tab: four people, first in first out.
- Back on the Overview, click **Invite all 4 from reserve**. "They now show in Sessions as invited, the gap closes, and the verdict improves."
- Note: people sourced after launch never go straight into an interview. They are screened into the reserve first.

**4. Escalation (2 min)**
"In an AI interview a participant can ask for a human. We offer it to standby moderators one at a time. If nobody accepts, it lands here as Waiting for moderator, with a 24-hour promise."
- On the waiting row (P-2042), click **Assign moderator**, pick someone, and confirm.
- Open **Sessions**, choose the **Escalated** filter. "Five escalations, and each shows how it ended."
- Open P-2045: offered to two moderators, nobody answered, I assigned Priya, she joined.
- Open P-2050: the participant chose to leave instead of waiting. Open P-2047: the participant booked a follow-up.

**5. Drill down and hand-off (1 min)**
"Every number opens the sessions behind it. A session shows what happened to one person. Finished interviews go to Review, where I decide whether the data is usable. That is the button at the bottom: Review N sessions."
